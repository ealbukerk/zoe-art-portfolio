About The Artist

Zoe

Zoe is a multidisciplinary artist whose practice moves between painting, drawing, fashion development, and printmaking. Her work often begins with collected fragments such as quick sketches, fabric samples, found colors, and observations from daily life, which are then transformed into layered pieces that balance structure with emotion.

Across every medium, Zoe is interested in how texture can hold memory and how visual design can carry a feeling before a viewer even reads the story behind it. This portfolio brings together studio work that reflects curiosity, experimentation, and a strong attention to detail, while leaving room for the process to stay visible inside the finished piece.
